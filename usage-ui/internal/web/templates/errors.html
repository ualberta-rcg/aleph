Reading these numbers

  • Model wake-ups ({{num .Totals.ColdStarts}} in this range) are not counted as errors. They are attempts that found the model not running: the gateway may have started it, or refused because no capacity was free. Retrying after a short wait often succeeds; repeated wake-ups can mean the platform is busy.
  • The meanings are the usual causes. The response your client received has the exact reason.
  • If an error keeps happening and the meaning does not explain it, contact support@tech.alliancecan.ca with the model, the time (UTC), and the status.
